Export Logistics

Door-to-door container service, two ways to move.

Two ways to move your ocean freight: empty containers are loaded directly at our Corpus Christi plant and trucked to Seaboard Marine's terminal for export — or product moves by truck to the Port of Houston, where it's transloaded into containers. Either way, freight moves door to door, with no extra handling in between.

ROUTE ONE — TRUCK & TRANSLOAD
Transload at the Port of Houston
